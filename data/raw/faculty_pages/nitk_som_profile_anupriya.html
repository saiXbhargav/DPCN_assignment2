Anupriya

Designation: 

Assistant Professor

Date of Joining at NITK: 

Thursday, February 20, 2025

Professional Experience: 

1. February 2025–Present Assistant Professor, School of Humanities, Social Sciences and Management, National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal.
2. July 2024–January 2025 Assistant Professor (Contractual), Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology, Jalandhar.
3. 2021–2022 Research Scholar, Department of Forensic Medicine and Toxicology, All India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi. Research on linguistic analysis and natural language processing of suicide notes to investigate psychological markers; supervised by Dr. Chittaranjan Behera.
4. 2016–2019 Teaching Assistant and Course Instructor, Centre for Linguistics, Jawaharlal Nehru University, New Delhi. Developed and taught undergraduate electives: Linguistics, Language and Literature (2019); Language Learning (2018); Language in Society (2017). Course coordinator: Prof. Hari Madhav Ray.
