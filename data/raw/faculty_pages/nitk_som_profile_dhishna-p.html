Achievements

1. Athira B and Dr. Dhishna Pannikot received the best paper award for the coauthored paper with Athira B, titled " Multiple Sovereignties and Necropolitics in Northeast India: Analysing the Conflict Narrative in Mitra Phukan's The Collector's Wife and Veio Pou’s Waiting for the Dust to Settle" presented at the ATHENA-2023 International Conference, held during 5-7 December 2023, organized by the School of Social Sciences and Humanities at VIT-AP University, Andhra Pradesh.
2. Dr. Dhishna P received Sri Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan “The Best Teacher award in English and Comparative Literature” on 5th Sept 2021 organized by CPACE and IMRF, Andrapradesh, India.
3. Dr. Dhishna P received The Venus International Foundation award of “Young Woman in Language Studies” for the contribution and achievement in the field of English Language on 4th March 2017 organized by VIF, Centre for Women Empowerment at Chennai, Tamil Nadu India.
4. Dr. Dhishna P received the Best Paper Award for the paper entitled “Connotations of Culture and Identity: A Study on the Significance of Cross-Cultural Interactions” in the international Conference on "Identity, Culture and Communication (ICICC 2016)" sponsored by International Economics and Development Research Centre (IEDRC), which was held from 12th to 14th November, 2016 at Kuta Central Park Hotel, Bali, Indonesia.
5. Tanupriya and Dhishna P received the Best Paper Award for the paper entitled “Exploring Kinesthetics of Gender Identity through Select Transgender Autobiographies.” in the International Journal of English and Literature (IJEL) 2016.
6. Dr. Dhishna P won Rev Father Lawerence Sundharam Gold Medal for the Best Meritorious Ph D Scholar in English, Pondicherry Central University, 2010.
